Two machines, one workflow.

A portable studio and a used-parts workstation. The Mac is where the experiments are made; the 2080 Ti pair is where the models come home to.

Two cards ≠ one pool

44 GB total, 22 GB each. No NVLink: tensor splits cross PCIe, and the KV cache takes a line item. The second card is memory with a commute.

Good at

7B–32B quantized inference, embeddings, classification, private batches and small LoRA runs.

Not good at

70B at usable context, frontier reasoning, long-context research and image or video generation at speed.

Built from used parts: 2 × Xeon E5-2696 v4, 2 × RTX 2080 Ti at 22 GB, 94 GB DDR4. Still cheaper than renting.
