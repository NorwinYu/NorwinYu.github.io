02 / Takeaways

What the competitions changed.

Three habits that survived the leaderboard and made the game work better.

01

Align the metric

Macro F1, balanced accuracy and online score reward different behaviour. Pick the loss and split that match the metric, not the leaderboard folklore.

02

Build the split first

Prompt-level splits and seed checks catch more leakage than another model. A clean negative result is information.

03

Keep the failures

The feature that did not replicate and the loss that did not help are part of the result. They are also the most useful interview material.
